ABOUT

Your pool starts with what you already play.

LoL Champion Pool organizes recent Summoner's Rift matches by champion and role to show observable familiarity and help you build a small set of picks.

Personal signal

The signal uses recent volume, KDA and results from your own sample. It is not a tier list and does not predict future wins.

Data

Lookup goes through the server-side gamer backend. No Riot key is exposed in the browser.
